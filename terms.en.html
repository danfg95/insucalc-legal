2. Description of service

InsuCalc is a nutritional support tool for people with diabetes that facilitates the calculation of carbohydrate portions from the nutritional information of meals, following a method based on carbohydrate, fat and protein content. It also offers premium features: AI-powered food photo analysis, a food search tool and a barcode scanner. Portion calculation is free.

InsuCalc does not calculate medication doses, is not a medical device and performs no clinical function. The result provided by the app is an indicative nutritional figure, not a therapeutic recommendation.
